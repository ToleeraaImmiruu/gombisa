PUT YOUR PHOTOS HERE
====================
hero.jpg   -> big picture at the top of the home page   (about 1920 x 1000, landscape)
about.jpg  -> banner picture on the About page          (about 1920 x 800, landscape)

Use .jpg, .webp or .png (the site tries them in that order).
Keep each file under about 400 KB (compress at squoosh.app).
Only use photos you own or have permission to use, and have consent from the people shown.
If a file is missing, the site shows a black, red and white woven placeholder instead.
